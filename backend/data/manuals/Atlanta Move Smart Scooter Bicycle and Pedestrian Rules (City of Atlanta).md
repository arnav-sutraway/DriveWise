# City of Atlanta: Move Smart, An Enforcement Officer's Guide to Creating Safer Streets for People Who Walk, Bike, or Scoot in Atlanta

Source: City of Atlanta Office of Mobility Planning flyer "Move Smart" (https://www.atlantaga.gov/home/showdocument?id=42502), retrieved from the Internet Archive copy captured December 20, 2022, because atlantaga.gov blocks automated downloads. This is a text transcription of the two-page flyer that keeps its "Violations" and "Allowed" groupings; the original PDF is kept in data/manuals/sources/. "Sec 150-xxx" citations are to the Atlanta City Code of Ordinances; "§ 40-x-xxx" citations are to the Official Code of Georgia (O.C.G.A.).

## Scooters (regulated by Atlanta Ordinance 18-O-1322)

Scooter violations in Atlanta:
- Riding a scooter on the sidewalk is a violation. [Atlanta Sec 150-405(b)]
- Not yielding to pedestrians in all cases is a violation. [Atlanta Sec 150-405(g)]
- Riding with more than one person per device is a violation. [Atlanta Sec 150-403(f)]
- Using a wireless telecommunications device while riding is a violation. [Atlanta Sec 150-403(g)]

Allowed for scooters in Atlanta:
- Riding on the BeltLine and in City parks is allowed. [Atlanta Sec 150-405(a)]
- Riding on bike lanes, shared use paths, and in the street is allowed. [Atlanta Sec 150-405(c)]
- Riding without a helmet is allowed. [Atlanta Sec 150-403(e)]
- Wearing any type of clothing is allowed.

## Bicycles (regulated by Georgia and Atlanta Code)

Violations for motor vehicle drivers around bicycles:
- Not leaving three feet of space when passing, following, overtaking, and/or moving in front of a bicyclist. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-46(c)] [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-56]
- Parking in a bike lane or LIT lane. [Atlanta Code Sec. 150-65(b)(4)]

Violations for bikes:
- Riding on the sidewalks, unless under the age of 12. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144]
- Riding against traffic. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-294(f)]
- Riding at night without white front light and red rear reflector or light. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-296(a)]
- Riding on a shared use path in a reckless manner which endangers other trail users. [Atlanta Sec. 150-65(b)(6)]

Allowed for bikes:
- Using the entire travel lane. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-294]
- Riding 2 riders abreast. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-294(b)]
- Riding without a helmet, unless under the age of 16. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-296(d)]
- Wearing any type of clothing.

Note: The Atlanta Bicycle Coalition offers a Ticket Diversion Class for anyone who receives a bicycle-related ticket.

## Pedestrians (regulated by Georgia Code)

Violations for motor vehicle drivers around pedestrians:
- Not stopping for pedestrians in crosswalks. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-91(a)]
- Not stopping for the solid or flashing red phase of a Pedestrian Hybrid Beacon signal. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-21]
- Blocking a crosswalk. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-203]

Violations for pedestrians:
- Crossing the street outside a crosswalk when both adjacent intersections are signalized. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-92]

Allowed for pedestrians:
- Pedestrians may cross the street outside of a crosswalk IF one of the adjacent intersections is not signalized AND they yield to traffic. [O.C.G.A. § 40-6-92]
- Wearing any type of clothing.

## Background on shareable scooters and e-bikes (flyer page 2)

The Atlanta ordinance regulating Shareable Dockless Mobility Devices (i.e. shareable scooters and e-bikes) was approved by City Council in January 2019. It is currently in effect. Two major enforcement challenges are riding and parking behaviors.

Riding: The Atlanta Police Department and other law enforcement agencies are responsible for enforcing riding behavior. Ride in the bike/LIT lane or the street. Do NOT ride on the sidewalk.

Key rule: Penalties for riding on the sidewalk include a fine up to $1,000 and jail time up to six months. This is the default penalty for all municipal charges. The fine amount is at the discretion of the judge of the Municipal Court.

Parking: The Atlanta Department of Public Works identifies illegally parked scooters and impounds violating devices. Removal requests can also be directed to ATL311. Office of Mobility Planning: mobility@atlantaga.gov.

Reporting crashes: For crash reporting of non-car modes of transportation in Atlanta, a moped, scooter, or minibike is recorded as vehicle type 18 (unit type: Bike); a pedacycle or bicycle is vehicle type 19 (unit type: Bike); a pedestrian has vehicle type None (unit type: Ped).
