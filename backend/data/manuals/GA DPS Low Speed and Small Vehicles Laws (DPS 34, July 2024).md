# Georgia Department of Public Safety: Charging Low-Speed & Small Vehicles (DPS-34, July 2024)

Source: Georgia Department of Public Safety brochure "2024 Georgia Charging Low-Speed & Small Vehicles", form DPS-34 (07/2024), downloaded from https://dps.georgia.gov/document/document/dps-34-low-speed-vehicles-trifold-july-2024/download. This is a text transcription of the two-page brochure; the original PDF is kept in data/manuals/sources/. The page 2 table is transcribed one vehicle type at a time so each answer stays with its vehicle. Code citations are to the Official Code of Georgia Annotated (O.C.G.A.).

## Vehicle definitions (brochure page 1)

### Personal Transportation Vehicle (PTV), O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(43.4)
Any motor vehicle having no fewer than three wheels and an unladen weight of 1,300 pounds or less and which cannot operate at more than 20 miles per hour if such vehicle was authorized to operate on local roads by a local authority prior to January 1, 2012. Such vehicles may also be referred to as "motorized carts" in some local ordinances; AND any motor vehicle: (i) with a minimum of four wheels; (ii) capable of a maximum level ground speed of less than 20 miles per hour; (iii) with a maximum gross vehicle unladen or empty weight of 1,375 pounds; (iv) capable of transporting not more than eight persons. Does not include mobility aids, including electric personal assistive mobility devices, power wheelchairs, and scooters, that can be used indoors and outdoors for the express purpose of enabling mobility for a person with a disability. The term also does not include any all-terrain vehicle or multipurpose off-highway vehicle. (Example: vehicles similar in size and form to a golf cart but used for any purpose other than golf.)

### Golf Carts, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(17.3)
Any motorized vehicle designed for the purpose and exclusive use of conveying one or more persons and equipment to play the game of golf in an area designated as a golf course. For such vehicle to be considered a golf car or golf cart, its average speed shall be less than 15 miles per hour. (Note: if vehicle is used for any purpose other than golf on a golf course, it is not a golf cart under this definition.)

### Motorcycles, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(29)
Every motor vehicle having a seat or saddle for the use of the rider and designed to travel on not more than three wheels in contact with the ground, but excluding a tractor, all-terrain vehicle and moped. Motorcycles and mopeds also fit within a larger class of vehicles defined as "motor driven cycles" by 40-1-1(30).

### Mopeds, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(28)
Moped: A motor driven cycle equipped with two or three wheels, with or without foot pedals to permit muscular propulsion, and an independent power source providing a maximum of two brake horsepower. If a combustion engine is used, the maximum piston or rotor displacement shall be 3.05 cubic inches (50 cubic centimeters) regardless of the number of chambers in such power source. The power source shall be capable of propelling the vehicle, unassisted, at a speed not to exceed 30 miles per hour (48.28 kilometers per hour) on level road surface and shall be equipped with a power drive system that functions directly or automatically only, not requiring clutching or shifting by the operator after the drive system is engaged.

Note: Electric rideshare scooters (e.g., "Bird" and "Lime" scooters) generally meet the definition of a moped.

### Bicycles and Electric Assisted Bicycles, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(6) and (15.3)
40-1-1(6) Bicycle: Every device propelled by human power upon which any person may ride, having only two wheels which are in tandem and either of which is more than 13 inches in diameter.
40-1-1(15.3) Electric assisted bicycle (EAB): A device with two or three wheels which has a saddle and fully operative pedals for human propulsion and also has an electric motor having a power output of not more than 750 watts.
40-6-300: Classes of EABs.

### Multipurpose Off-Highway Vehicle, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(33.1)
Any motorized vehicle having features specifically intended for utility use and having the following characteristics: (A) Has the capability to transport persons or cargo or both; (B) Operates between 25 miles per hour (40.2 kilometers per hour) and 65 miles per hour (104.6 kilometers per hour); (C) Has an overall width of 80 inches (2,030 millimeters) or less, exclusive of accessories or attachments; (D) Is designed to travel on four or more wheels; (E) Uses a steering wheel for steering control; (F) Contains a nonstraddle seat; (G) Has a gross vehicle weight rating of less than 4,000 pounds (1,814 kilograms); and (H) Has a minimum cargo capacity of 350 pounds (159 kilograms).

### All-Terrain Vehicles, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(3)
A motorized vehicle originally manufactured for off-highway use which is equipped with three or more nonhighway tires, is 80 inches or less in width with a dry weight of 3,500 pounds or less, and is designed for or capable of cross-country travel on or immediately over land, water, snow, ice, marsh, swampland, or other natural terrain.

### Low-Speed Vehicles, O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(25.1)
Any four-wheeled vehicle whose top speed attainable in one mile is greater than 20 miles per hour but not greater than 25 miles per hour on a paved level surface and which is manufactured or converted to comply with standards based upon those federal motor vehicle safety standards for low-speed vehicles set forth in 49 C.F.R. Section 571.500, as amended.

### Electric Personal Assistive Mobility Devices (EPAMD), O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(15.4)
A self-balancing, two non-tandem wheeled device designed to transport only one person and having an electric propulsion system with average power of 750 watts (1 horsepower) and a maximum speed of less than 20 miles per hour on a paved level surface when powered solely by such propulsion system and ridden by an operator who weighs 170 pounds. (Example: vehicle commonly referred to as a "Segway".)

## Laws applicable to classes of low-speed and small vehicles while operating on the highway (brochure page 2)

Review the definitions above to determine what type of vehicle is applicable. Vehicles operated OFF the highway (as defined by O.C.G.A. § 40-1-1(19) as the entire width between the boundary lines of every way publicly maintained when any part thereof is open to the use of the public for purposes of vehicular travel) are not subject to these laws but may be subject to other laws or local ordinances.

### Moped laws (includes electric rideshare scooters such as Bird and Lime, which generally meet the moped definition)
- Registration required? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-2-20(b)(6).
- Driver's license / age requirement? License OR permit required. Must be 15 or older. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-351.
- Proof of insurance required? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
- DUI applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391.
- Implied consent applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1(a).
- Child seats required? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.
- Operable on sidewalk? NO, except upon driveways. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144.
- Limitations on highway operation? State/local DOT can prohibit on roads as needed. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-54.
- Lighting requirements: Same as other motorcycles BUT not required to illuminate head/tail light at all times. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-350, 40-8-20 through 40-8-35.
- Other requirements / limitations: See O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-350 through 354.

### Bicycle / Electric Assisted Bicycle (EAB) laws
- Registration required? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-2-20; 40-6-291(a).
- Driver's license / age requirement? No age or license required for bicycle or Class I or II EAB. Must be 15 or older BUT no license required for Class III EAB. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-20; 40-6-303.
- Proof of insurance required? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
- DUI applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391; must also charge 40-6-291.
- Implied consent applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390; must also charge 40-6-291.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1(a).
- Child seats required? YES, see code for requirements. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-292.
- Operable on sidewalk? NO, except upon driveways and children under 12 by ordinance. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144.
- Limitations on highway operation? NO, unless prohibited by DOT / sign. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-54.
- Lighting requirements: Headlight and taillight OR rear reflector required at night. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-296.
- Other requirements / limitations: See O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-290 through 298 (bicycles); 40-6-300 through 303 (EABs).

### Electric Personal Assistive Mobility Device (EPAMD, e.g. Segway) laws
- Registration required? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-2-20; 40-1-1(15.4).
- Driver's license / age requirement? No license required. Must be 16 or older on highway. No age requirement on sidewalk. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-325(b).
- Proof of insurance required? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
- DUI applicable? YES, BUT charge under O.C.G.A. § 40-6-326.
- Implied consent applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1.
- Child seats required? Not applicable. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.
- Operable on sidewalk? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-320.
- Limitations on highway operation? Only where speed limit is 35 mph or less or a bike lane exists. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-320.
- Lighting requirements: Front, rear, side reflectors; headlight for night operation. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-325.
- Other requirements / limitations: See O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-320 through 327.

### Motorcycle laws
- Registration required? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-2-20.
- Driver's license / age requirement? Class M license required (except "Slingshots", which require Class C). O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-20; 40-5-23.
- Proof of insurance required? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-11.
- DUI applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391.
- Implied consent applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1(a).
- Child seats required? NO, but see seating requirements of O.C.G.A. § 40-6-311. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.
- Operable on sidewalk? NO, except upon driveways. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144.
- Limitations on highway operation? NO, unless prohibited by DOT / sign. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-54.
- Lighting requirements: Same as other motor-driven cycles AND required to illuminate head/tail light at all times. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-312, 40-8-20 through 40-8-35.
- Other requirements / limitations: See O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-310 through 316.

### Personal Transportation Vehicle (PTV) laws
Requirements for PTVs may be altered by local ordinance, especially if ordinances existed prior to January 1, 2012.
- Registration required? NO if electric; YES if gas powered. O.C.G.A. § 40-2-20(b)(5).
- Driver's license / age requirement? License required UNLESS exempt under ordinance. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-20; 40-6-331.
- Proof of insurance required? NO if electric; YES if gas powered. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
- DUI applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391.
- Implied consent applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1(c)(6).
- Child seats required? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.
- Operable on sidewalk? NO, except upon driveways. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144.
- Limitations on highway operation? Authorized only where speed limit is 25 mph or less. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-369.1.
- Lighting requirements: Headlights, taillights, reflectors required, subject to same requirements as other motor vehicles. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-330.1; 40-8-20 through 40-8-35.
- Other requirements / limitations: See O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-330 through 331 and 363 through 369.1.

### All-terrain vehicle (ATV) laws
- Registration required? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-2-20.
- Driver's license / age requirement? License required. O.C.G.A. § 40-5-20.
- Proof of insurance required? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
- DUI applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391.
- Implied consent applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-5-55; 40-5-67.1.
- Reckless driving applicable? YES. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-390.
- Seatbelt requirements applicable? POSSIBLY: required if capable of a top speed of greater than 20 mph AND not in use in connection with a farming operation by an operator 18 years of age or older. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.1.
- Child seats required? NO. O.C.G.A. § 40-8-76.
- Operable on sidewalk? NO, except upon driveways. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-144.
- Limitations on highway operation? NO, unless prohibited by DOT / sign. O.C.G.A. § 40-6-54.
- Lighting requirements: Same as other motor vehicles. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-8-20 through 40-8-35.
- Other requirements / limitations: Generally subject to all rules of the road if operated on highway (see Op. Att'y Gen. No. 2007-3).

- Driver's license / age requirement? License required. O.C.G.A. § 40-5-20.
- Proof of insurance required? YES. O.C.G.A. §§ 40-6-10; 33-34-4.
